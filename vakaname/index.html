Vakaname

Vakaname is a publishing tool that produces short history and mystery videos for the Vakaname YouTube channel and uploads them to the channel. It is developed and operated by M. Levent Sipahi (Mulesi Games).

What it does

Vakaname uploads finished videos to the YouTube channel together with their title, description, tags and thumbnail, schedules their publishing time and reads basic statistics (such as views) of the uploaded videos. It accesses the Google account only with the channel owner's permission, through Sign in with Google (OAuth). The tool is used only by the channel owner; there is no public sign-up or user account.

Google data

Vakaname uses the information it receives from Google APIs only for the tasks above; it does not sell it, use it for advertising or share it with others. See the Privacy Policy for details and the Terms of Service for the conditions of use.
